# ACDC Research Group @ UNSW Sydney

Welcome to the GitHub organization of the **ACDC Research Group** at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Sydney.

## About Us

The ACDC Research Group focuses on advanced photovoltaic (PV) research and development at UNSW Sydney, one of the world's leading universities in solar cell research and renewable energy technologies.

## Our Website

Visit our official website for more information about our research, team, publications, and projects:

🌐 **[www.acdc-pv-unsw.com](https://www.acdc-pv-unsw.com/)**

## Research Focus

Our research group is dedicated to advancing photovoltaic technology through:
- Innovative solar cell designs and materials
- Advanced characterization and testing methods
- Sustainable energy solutions
- Collaboration with industry and academic partners

## Get Involved

We welcome collaboration and inquiries from researchers, industry partners, and students interested in photovoltaic research.
